Yes, we're a salon — and so much more

Botanica is the Gold Coast's largest freelance-only hair salon, and an exceptional venue for industry events: product launches, educational evenings, networking nights and celebrations. The feel is industrial luxe with natural elements throughout, creating a calm, photogenic backdrop you can make entirely your own.

Events can run on any day, at any time. Our team helps you plan the layout, coordinates on the night, and handles the set-up and pack-down so you can focus on your guests.

Facilities

The venue

  • A beautiful 300m² space, surrounded by a backdrop of greenery
  • Large welcome area
  • Two lounge areas
  • Two catering rooms for professional caterers
  • Bathrooms on site, disability accessible
  • Rear roller-door access for easy load-in
  • Plenty of free parking

Inclusions

What comes with it

  • Catering serving-ware, platters and display tiers
  • Glassware — champagne, wine and tumblers
  • Champagne buckets and ice tubs (ice to be provided)
  • Tables and tablecloths for drinks stations
  • 16 low stools and seats
  • Decorations*
  • Sonos music system, or Bluetooth your own playlist
  • A staff member to coordinate before and during your event
  • Space preparation before and after the event
  • Cleaning and rubbish removal

*Decoration options vary — we'll confirm what's available for your date.
